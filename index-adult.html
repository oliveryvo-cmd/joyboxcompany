How it works

Two boxes. One order.

No membership, no subscription, no fine print. You buy a box, a kid gets a box.

01

You pick a box

Smile, Giggle, Sunshine or Rainbow. Each one is a different size of surprise.

02

We pack it by hand

Every box is assembled individually. Nothing is drop-shipped, nothing is automated.

03

A Joy Box goes out

Part of what you paid funds a second box — toys, games, and a note written by hand — for a disabled kid.

04

You hear back

We keep a public count of Joy Boxes delivered. You can check it any time. It's on this page.
